What it does for your customers

Every reply is filled in from your own data, so it does not invent a price or a policy. The small AI only decides what the customer wants.

Answers questions

Opening hours, delivery, returns, anything you put in the FAQ sheet, in your own words.

Catalog and prices

Customers ask "do you have honey?" and get your products with photo, price and stock.

Takes orders

"add 2 raw honey" builds the order. It counts only after the customer replies YES.

Delivery slots

Customers pick from your slots, each with a limit you set.

Service bookings

Salons, tutors, clinics and repair visits: customers book a day and time, and you see it on a calendar.

How to pay

Your payment link, QR code image, bank transfer details or UPI, with the invoice. You confirm the money with one click and they get a receipt.

Order and booking status

"where is my order?" gets that customer's own status, and every change you make is sent to them.

Hands over to you

When it is not sure, or someone asks for a person, the customer is told you will reply, and the message waits for you.

Stays polite

Only answers people who wrote first, at a human pace, with a daily cap. Never broadcasts.

Why it costs nothing

Your own computer is the server. You choose where customers can reach you.

  • No AI subscription. A small AI model is built in and runs on your computer. Your chats are not sent to an AI company.
  • No paid auto-reply service. It links to WhatsApp and Telegram directly.
  • No hosting, no domain. The shop page and the web chat are served from your computer.
  • Open source. The code is published under the MIT license at github.com/ownshopai/ownshopai. No charges for the software, ever.
  • Your data stays with you. Orders, products and chats are files on your computer.

Honest limits

Read these before you rely on it.

Your computer has to stay on and online

When it is off, asleep or offline, nobody gets a reply. Messages that arrived meanwhile are not answered in a burst later.

The WhatsApp link is unofficial

WhatsApp is linked through an unofficial client, and WhatsApp may block numbers that use one. Use a spare number. Telegram uses the official Bot API.

English only for now

Ordering and replies work in English. The model is small: in our test it picked the right action for 30 of 44 customer messages. When it is not sure, the message comes to you.

You check payments yourself

OwnShop AI never touches money and is not connected to a payment provider. You look at your bank or wallet and click Payment received.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no plan and no AI bill: the model runs on your computer. The only running cost is keeping your computer on and online.

Is my data safe?

Your products, orders and chats stay in files on your computer, and the AI runs there too. Messages travel only through WhatsApp, Telegram or, for the web chat link, Cloudflare, between your customers and your computer. Keep your computer itself secure and backed up.

Will WhatsApp ban my number?

It can. The WhatsApp link is unofficial, and WhatsApp may block numbers that use unofficial clients. OwnShop AI only answers people who wrote first, at a human pace, but that is no guarantee. Use a spare number, or use Telegram, which is official.

Do I need a website or domain?

No. Customers can reach you on WhatsApp or Telegram, or on your own web chat link, with a free fixed link like name.ownshopai.store, through the OwnShop AI link service (nothing to install). Your computer has to stay on for the link to answer, and the link service and Cloudflare carry that traffic. See Put your chat on the web.

Do I need a technical person?

No. You download one file, double-click it, and do the setup in your browser. If you can edit an Excel sheet, you can run it. The manual has every step with screenshots.

What computer do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC (x64 or ARM64) or a Mac with Apple silicon, with about 1.5 GB free disk space, about 1 GB of free memory while it runs, and an internet connection. It must stay on while you want customers answered.

Does it take payments?

No. It sends your payment details with the invoice: a payment link, a QR code image, bank transfer text or UPI. You check that the money arrived and click Payment received.

I sell services, not products. Does it work?

Yes. Choose Services in the first setup step and pick your kind of business. Customers write things like "book Haircut" and pick a day and time.
